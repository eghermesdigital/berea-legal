Privacy Policy

Berea: Christian Devotional

Effective September 1, 2026

👋 Overview

Berea: Christian Devotional ("we", "our", or "us") is developed by EG Digital. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our app.

The short version: Berea collects nothing. The app makes no network requests of its own, contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and does not ask you to create an account. Everything you write stays on your device.

📦 Information We Collect

None. Berea has no account, no login and no server of its own. It does not collect usage analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers or a notification token, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Two things do exist, and neither is collected by us:

  • What you write — reflections, prayer intentions and notebook entries live on your own device. If you have iCloud turned on, they are also saved to your own private iCloud account, so you do not lose them when you change phones. That copy belongs to you: it is protected by your Apple Account, we cannot read, list or recover it, and you can turn it off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings › iCloud.
  • Purchases — if you buy Berea Plus, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details, and we receive no personal data from the purchase.

Daily reminders are local notifications, scheduled by the app on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, and no push token exists.

⚙️ How We Use Your Information

We have no information to use. We do not profile you, we do not show behavioural advertising, and there is nothing for us to sell to anyone.

🔗 Third-Party Services

None. Berea bundles no third-party SDK — no analytics, no attribution, no crash reporting, no advertising. Purchases go through Apple's own StoreKit, and iCloud backup uses Apple's CloudKit into your private database; both are Apple services governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

🗓️ Data Retention

We hold no data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. What you write stays on your device — deleting the app removes it. If iCloud backup is on, the copy in your private iCloud account stays under your control and disappears when you delete it or turn the backup off.

👶 Children's Privacy

Berea is not directed to children under the age of 4. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

⚖️ Your Rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the LGPD give you rights over personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to a supervisory authority. In our case there is nothing to exercise them against: we hold no personal data about you.

What you wrote in the app is yours and under your control — delete an entry in the app, or delete the app, and it is gone; the iCloud copy, if you enabled it, lives in your own Apple Account and is removed the same way. If you have any question about this, write to sos@egdigital.com and we will answer within 30 days.

🔒 Security

The safest data is the data that is never collected, and that is the design here: Berea sends nothing to us, so there is no database of ours to breach. What stays on your device is protected by iOS itself, and the optional iCloud copy is protected by your Apple Account.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Effective" date at the top of this page.

Continued use of Berea after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
