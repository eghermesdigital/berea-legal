📖 Berea

Christian Devotional

🌅 A verse, a reflection, a minute of prayer

Berea gives you one Scripture verse a day, a short written reflection, and a guided minute of prayer. There is a notebook for what you want to keep, a streak for the days you show up, and a widget so the verse meets you on your Lock Screen.

No account. No ads. No tracking. It works with the phone in airplane mode — the app makes no network requests of its own, and what you write stays on your device.

The name comes from Acts 17:11 — the Bereas, who received the word eagerly and examined the Scriptures daily to see whether these things were so. That daily habit is the whole idea.
