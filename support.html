❓ Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I restore my subscription? Go to Settings → Berea Plus → Restore Purchases. Your subscription is linked to your Apple ID.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Open your iPhone's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Berea → Cancel. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.
  • What's the difference between Monthly and Lifetime? Monthly renews automatically at $5.99/month after a 3-day free trial. Lifetime is a one-time $39.99 purchase that unlocks every feature forever, with no recurring charge.
  • How do I enable daily reminders? Go to Settings → Daily Reminder and toggle it on. You can choose your preferred time to receive your daily verse.
  • Is my devotional data private? Absolutely. All your reflections and journal entries are stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud account. EG Digital has no server and can never read your devotional content.
  • How does the daily streak work? Open the app each day and complete your devotional (read the verse, reflect, or pray). Your streak grows every consecutive day you participate.
